# Questions for the AI Analytics Agent

## Dataset Overview

1. How many records are in the dataset?
2. What columns are available?
3. Are there missing values?
4. What are the main characteristics of the data?

## Trend Analysis

5. What trends can be identified?
6. Which category performs best?
7. Which category performs worst?
8. How does performance change over time?

## Business Insights

9. What are the three most important insights?
10. Which areas require improvement?
11. Are there any unusual patterns?
12. What factors might explain the observed trends?

## Business Recommendations

13. How can performance be improved?
14. Which categories should receive more attention?
15. What actions should the business prioritize?

## Reporting

16. Summarize the analysis in simple language.
17. Prepare a concise business report.
18. Format the findings for an email.
19. List the key findings and recommendations.
20. Identify questions that require further investigation.

Note: Actual capabilities depend on the connected dataset,
available tools, and workflow configuration.
